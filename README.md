# Chirpie

Chirpie is an AI-native news companion that transforms current events into personalized, chat-style digests. Instead of browsing dense article feeds, users receive news in a conversational format with customizable tone, themes, and follow-up exploration. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

## Overview

Chirpie is designed to make staying informed feel as easy as checking your messages. The product combines conversational delivery, aesthetic UI, and interactive follow-up so users can move from “just tell me the gist” to “tell me more” without leaving the chat experience. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Early positioning is centered on:
- General News
- Pop Culture
- Finance

The MVP focuses on a chat-first daily digest, onboarding personalization, and a design-forward experience that feels warm, calm, and habit-forming. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2} :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

## Problem

Traditional news products are built around feeds and articles, but many users now prefer:
- shorter updates
- conversational delivery
- personalized tone
- lower-friction ways to stay informed

Chirpie rethinks news consumption around conversation, delight, and return behavior rather than just summarization. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

## MVP Features

- Public landing page
- Sign up / sign in flow
- Onboarding for interests, tone, and style preferences
- Chat-style digest experience
- Daily and weekly digest modes
- Follow-up Q&A
- Source link-outs
- Save and share actions
- Theme selection
- Account / preferences page :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

## Product Principles

- Fast before deep
- Cute, not childish
- Source-aware
- Personalization is about format too
- Conversation should unlock depth

## Design Direction

Chirpie is visually inspired by soft pastel interfaces, rounded forms, pixel-accent moments, and subtle motion. The interface is intended to feel airy, warm, and playful without losing credibility. Motion should feel alive, not noisy. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

## Vision

Chirpie aims to become a trusted daily companion for staying informed through:
- personalized conversational delivery
- strong visual identity
- calm daily ritual behavior
- source-aware trust design :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}
